Wall-E

Wall-E is a personal assistant operated for a small household. It helps its operator and invited household members organize preferences, tasks and reminders.

With the account holder’s explicit authorization, its Google integration can search and read Gmail messages and read Google Calendar events and availability. The current integration requests read-only access. It cannot send email or create, change or delete calendar events through this integration.

Selected information may be processed by a cloud AI provider to answer the authorized person’s request. Durable household records are maintained on the operator’s local computer.

The service is not open for public registration. Access to a Google account requires that account holder’s consent.

Privacy policy

Privately operated household assistant. No public sign-up.
