Wall-E privacy policy

Updated October 5, 2026.

This policy describes the personal, privately operated Wall-E household assistant and its Google integration. It is intended for the operator and the limited household members personally invited to use it.

Information and access

Google authorization supplies account identity and permission to read Gmail and Google Calendar. Authorized requests may retrieve email identifiers, dates, senders, subjects and selected message content, or calendar event details and availability. OAuth read-only permissions may cover the mailbox and accessible calendars broadly; application query limits do not narrow the underlying Google grant to particular labels or events.

Use and processing

Information is used to fulfill authorized personal-assistant requests. Access is on demand, with bounded queries. Selected content can be sent to the configured cloud AI provider, currently OpenAI, for inference. Local storage does not mean that all information stays on the local computer. Information is not sold or used for advertising by this personal application.

Storage and access controls

Google authorization tokens are stored using macOS Keychain. Household records and assistant runtime state are stored on the operator’s local computer. Selected information may appear in local conversation history or records and in the cloud provider’s processing or retention systems. Those providers’ account controls and policies apply. The Google connector is restricted to the authorized account holder; a shared assistant and operating-system account are not a guarantee of complete information isolation between household members.

Retention, correction and disconnection

The operator can inspect, correct and delete local records and manages runtime-history retention. The account holder can revoke Wall-E’s Google access through their Google Account permissions. Revocation stops future authorized Google API access but does not automatically erase previously stored local records, backups or provider-held copies. These require their respective deletion controls; immediate or complete provider deletion is not promised.

Contact and changes

Contact the project owner using the support email displayed in the Google consent configuration. The operator will update this policy when the integration’s data access or use materially changes.
